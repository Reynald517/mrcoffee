Our Founder:

Susanto Liman

Born in 1982 to a family with deep Indonesian roots, Susanto Liman grew up surrounded by the rich aromas of freshly brewed coffee. Inspired by his heritage and a lifelong passion for crafting the perfect cup of coffee, he set out to share his love with the world. In 2005, he founded a small coffee shop which the world know today as Mr.Coffee.
